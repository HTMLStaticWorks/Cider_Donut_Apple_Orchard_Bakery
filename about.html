Cultivating Tradition
Since 1952

From a small roadside stand to a beloved valley institution, our journey has always been about one thing: the perfect apple.

A Legacy of Love

Arthur Miller planted the first seedling on this hillside over 70 years ago. He believed that the richest soil and the most patient hands would produce fruit that tasted like sunshine and heritage.

Today, the fourth generation of Millers continues that mission. We've grown from 10 acres to over 200, but our commitment to artisanal quality and community connection remains unchanged.

The Art of the Donut

It's not just a donut; it's a labor of love that takes 12 hours from press to pastry.

1. Fresh Pressing

We press our apples daily to create the unfiltered cider that forms the heart of our dough.

2. Slow Ferment

Our dough rests overnight to develop a complex flavor profile and that signature airy texture.

3. Hearth Fired

Fried in small batches and hand-tossed in our proprietary cinnamon-sugar blend while still hot.

Grown Responsibly

We don't just farm for today; we farm for the next century. Our sustainability initiatives ensure that the valley stays green for generations to come.

Solar Powered Bakery
